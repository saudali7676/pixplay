# Editor: Video
Reports to: CEO

## The job
- Delivers the edited video for each recording, in two passes.

## How
- Tools and files: raw footage, the Writer's script, [editing tool and export folder — TBD], the board.
- Rules: company rules in [README.md](README.md). Pass 1 is cut, pacing, and structure; pass 2 is captions, audio, and polish.

## Handoff
- Receives from: Owner (raw footage) and Writer (script), via the CEO
- Hands off to: CEO → Publisher

## "Done" checklist
- [ ] Pass 1 done: cut, pacing, and structure.
- [ ] Pass 2 done: captions, audio, and final polish.
- [ ] Final file exported and added to `recent` on the board; `report` event logged, status **Free**.
